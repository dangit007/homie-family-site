Questions

The things parents ask us first.

Is this another calendar to keep up to date?

It’s the opposite. You tell Homie things once, in a sentence, and it works out what that means for the week.

Will it put things in without asking?

Never. Homie shows you what it understood and waits. A person always decides.

Do the kids need phones?

No. They can be in the family without being on a phone, and you can add them later when they get one.

What about grandparents and babysitters?

Invite them with a code and give them as little or as much of the week as you want. It costs them nothing.

Does it work on the road?

Yes. Everything opens and edits with no signal and catches up when you’re back.

When is Android coming?

It’s in the plan. Leave your email on the download page and we’ll tell you the day it’s ready.

Who pays in a family?

One person, whoever set the household up. Everyone else joins for free.

Can I try it before paying?

On your own phone Homie is free for good. Sharing with the family is free for the first month.
