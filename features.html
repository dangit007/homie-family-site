Features

Homie helps guide your household

“Can we fit it?”

The question the app is built around. Homie looks at the week the way you would, and answers honestly, including the times the answer is no.

Leo’s been asked camping Aug 12–15. Can we fit it?
Yes — if 3 things giveMaya’s Thursday swim gets skipped, the nanny’s Friday is off, and Leo’s piano needs a ride.
Add to Plans · +3 changes

Talk, don’t type

Speak a plan while you’re making dinner. Your voice is turned into words on your own phone and never leaves it.

Real travel time

Homie knows how far the field is and how long it takes at that hour, and adds a little slack so arriving isn’t a sprint.

Conflicts in family terms

Not “overlapping events”. Nobody free to drive Leo, or a pickup that lands in the middle of your meeting.

Routines that repeat themselves

Bedtime, school runs, the nanny on Mondays, cleaners every other Thursday. Set them once and they hold their place in the week.

Who’s got them

Every plan says who is with the kids and who is doing the driving, so nobody assumes it was the other one.

Works without signal

In the car park, on the ferry, out at the cabin. Everything opens and edits offline and syncs up when you’re back.
