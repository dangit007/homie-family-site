Your family

Whoever helps you get through the week belongs in here.

A family is rarely just two parents and two kids. Homie fits the shape yours actually has.

Parents

Both hands on the wheel

Either of you can add anything, invite anyone and see the whole week. No main account, no second-class parent.

Kids and teens

Their day, at their level

Little ones see what’s next. Teens get their own week and can ask for a lift. Parents decide how much each one can change.

Grandparents and helpers

Just the days they’re needed

Grandma, the nanny, the neighbour who does Wednesdays. They see the runs they’re doing, and nothing else.
