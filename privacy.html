Privacy

Your family’s week is nobody’s business but yours.

Homie holds the most personal thing a family has: where everyone is, and when. There is no version of this app that sells that.

Your voice stays put

What you say is turned into words on your own phone. The recording is never uploaded and never stored.

Only your people

Your week is readable by the family members you’ve invited, and by nobody else. Not other households, not us to browse.

The helper forgets

When Homie answers a question it forgets what it read. Your family is never used to train anything.

Children come in through a parent

A child can only join when a parent invites them, and a parent decides what they can see and change. We ask children for nothing directly.

No ads, no trackers, no brokers

There is no advertising in Homie and no third party watching you use it. The app is paid for by families, which is the only business model that keeps it that way.
